How to read this table This is a research and comparison view, not a promise of profit. Look for a stronger signal, a healthy winning rate, realistic upside, and controlled downside together. Signal The model's overall suggestion. BUY / HOLD means the setup is strongest, WATCH means conditions are mixed, and AVOID means the setup is currently weak. Winning rate How often similar past setups reached their target before the stop. A higher percentage is generally better, but it is an estimate, not a guarantee. Target % The possible gain from the entry price if the target is reached. Higher may look attractive, but ambitious targets are harder to reach. Loss % The planned drop from entry to the stop-loss level. A smaller absolute percentage usually means more controlled risk. Trend score How many technical conditions agree with an upward trend. 4–5 is strong, 2–3 is mixed, and 0–1 is weak. Momentum The recent direction of price movement. Positive values show upward movement, negative values show downward movement, and values near zero show little movement. SMA alignment Compares today’s price with its 5, 20, 50, and 200-day averages. ▲ means price is above an average, ▼ means below it, and • means there is not enough data. More ▲ symbols usually indicate stronger alignment.
